# 066 — The merge question never recommends combining with an earlier copy

**Context.** Decision 007 asks before a share syncs into a folder that already holds files, and its Why names the legitimate case: a vault's own earlier data after it was removed and accepted again. The question turned that into its example — "for example after removing the vault, reopening VaultSync and accepting its share again". Removing and accepting again is also the way out #270 gives a vault that synced straight into the selected folder, where the folder of the vault's name is most likely one of the earlier copy's own folders, and the way on #315's card gives a vault whose folder was replaced by a copy. In both the folder is a copy, or part of one, and the card and the pending row said "do not combine" while the question said "accept". Combining with a copy can also send its older text of a note out as the newer one, without a conflict copy (#331).

**Decision.** The question names no way of reaching it as the case to accept. It says to accept only for the folder the vault synced in on this iPhone, unchanged since, or for notes the user wants to add; never for a copy, because an older version can replace the newer one on every device; and to give the vault another folder name with "Choose Vault…" otherwise. When the selected folder is an app's own folder with a `.obsidian/` of its own (`ObsidianVisibility.holdsEarlierCopy`), the question says the target is most likely part of that earlier copy and not to combine; the pending row uses the same rule before and after the tap. The confirmation itself stays (007), and so does its button.

**Why.** The text is read at the moment of the decision, by someone who arrived by exactly the route the example named. The app cannot tell the vault's own folder from a copy here without the bridge's record (decision 063), so the text has to hold for both.

**Rejected alternatives.** Refusing the merge in an earlier copy: a folder the user knows to be right would have no way in, which 007 rejects. Keeping the example with a warning beside it: the example is what people act on. Detecting a copy of the vault itself in the question from its marker: needs the bridge's record across a removal — #331's decision record decides what the app can do there.
